# Research Interests
I am broadly interested in both the theoretical and practical aspects of quantum information and computation, with a particular focus on Quantum Key Distribution (QKD). During my graduate studies, my work has primarily centered on the security analysis of practical QKD protocols. I remain deeply engaged with all aspects of QKD, especially security proofs and the pathway toward certification.

More recently, I have also begun exploring classical cryptography and the broader field of quantum cryptography.

I continue to do a little work on accurate descriptions of open quantum systems. This effort is towards understanding the validity and limitations of various quantum master equations, beyond specific system–bath models.
